# Finding a shift, and scoring an asset

The keys named here belong to the report: `references/report.md` explains those that need
it, and the example in `assets/direction-report.html` shows the rest.

- **Four places a real shift shows:** a law with a compliance date, money moving into teams
  or procurement, work advertised under no settled title yet, and a skill employers now pay
  extra for or newly ask for. Retirement curves are slower and more reliable than all four.
  Look once in each place, and where no shift survives say so under `sources.notConfirmed`:
  none found is a finding.
- **Name the mechanism, not the trend.** "This field will grow" asserts nothing. "This rule
  applies from that date, and somebody has to produce the evidence trail for it" can be
  checked, and points at who will be hiring. The source carries the date; the mechanism is
  reasoning from it, and is written as reasoning.
- **Say what would prove it wrong.** Take it from the source where one gives it, with its
  date. Where none does, write a threshold and a date yourself and label it as yours: the
  date is a quarter after the first date the shift names that is still ahead, or six months
  from the report's date if it names none. A quarter counts as its last day; a year or a
  period with no day names none. A threshold with no date is yours until it has one.
- **One source is said to be one.** A shift that rests on a single source says so at the end
  of `src`.
- **Ageing, by example.** Judgement, accountability, domain knowledge and being trusted with
  a system age well. Raw output speed, one vendor's tooling and being the newest at
  something age badly. These marks are judgements, and `horizon.note` says so. Each family's
  `ages` is the mark for the asset it buys, so families that buy the same asset carry the
  same mark, and `agesWell` and `agesBadly` list the skills behind the marks.
- **Two clocks, running together.** The fast one pays now and builds the record the slow one
  needs. The slow one is whatever cannot be bought later: a language, a qualification, a
  public record, a gap that only closes with repetition. Say which gates which in each
  lane's `sub`.
- **Put dates on the gates.** What closes, what enrols, what expires. A gate no source dates
  is written "date not established" in its step's `detail`, placed in its sequence on the
  lane, listed under `sources.notConfirmed`, and kept on the clock.
